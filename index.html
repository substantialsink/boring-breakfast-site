Boring Breakfast

An app for eating breakfast, not performing it.

Closed beta on Android — public release coming soon

Most food apps reward whoever stages the best photo. Boring Breakfast doesn't. It's built around a few rules instead:

  • Posting only happens in the morning, 7:00–11:30am. Outside that window, there's nothing to post.
  • You eat with a small, closed table of up to five people — not a feed of strangers.
  • There's one reaction: "I had that too." No likes, no followers, no counts.
  • Showing up most days counts for more than any single post.

These aren't missing features. They're the point.

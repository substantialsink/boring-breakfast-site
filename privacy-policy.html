Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Boring Breakfast is a small, independently-made app. This page explains what information it collects and how it's used.

What we collect

When you create an account (via Google sign-in or email and password), we collect your name and email address. When you use the app, we store the content you post — photos and messages — along with which "table" (your small group of up to five people) you belong to.

How it's used

This information is used only to run the app: showing your posts to your table, tracking your posting streak, and managing sign-in. We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with advertisers.

Third parties

The app relies on a small number of infrastructure providers to operate — including Google, for sign-in, and Expo, for app hosting and updates. These providers process data only as needed to provide those services.

Data deletion

You can request that your account and its data be deleted at any time by contacting us at askboringbreakfast@gmail.com.

Children's privacy

Boring Breakfast is not intended for use by children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and change the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to askboringbreakfast@gmail.com.
